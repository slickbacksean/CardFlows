Product

One loop, from the card to the draft.

Identify the print, decide what you will pay, and keep the copy in your own inventory. PokeSniper does not place bids, log into marketplaces, or publish listings for you.

Scan

Photograph one English raw single.

Fill the frame with the card. PokeSniper matches the art against the English catalog and shows you the print to confirm. You choose it. Nothing is written until you do.

Live shows

Read the card on the stream.

An overlay follows the card in live video and offers a guess. Confirm it yourself. The overlay is not a bid, and it does not scrape the show.

Max Buy

Your ceiling, calculated.

Margin, fees buffer, and condition. PokeSniper turns a reference price into the most you want to pay. It is your rule, not a market quote.

$84.00 × (1 − 0.20) × (1 − 0.13) = $58.46

Pre-grade

A photo estimate, not a cert.

Photograph the front and back before you send a card out. The result is a pre-grade estimate. Estimate only. Not an official grade.

Inventory

Purchased, or watching.

Save a copy with what you paid, or park it on a watchlist. Listing drafts stay in the app until you copy the title, condition, and asking price yourself.

Portfolio

A spread, not a promise.

See what you hold and the gap between all-in cost and asking price. Spreads are estimates. PokeSniper does not promise a profit.

How it works

Four steps. You stay in charge of each one.

  1. 01

    Point

    A photo of one card, or the live video in a show you are already watching.

  2. 02

    Confirm

    Check the print, set, and language. Reject it if the match is wrong.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Max Buy sits next to the reference price and the time that price was checked.

  4. 04

    Keep

    Purchased or watchlist. Draft a listing later. Copy it when you are ready.

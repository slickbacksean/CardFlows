## Full in-app disclaimer (draft)

**Card suggestions can be wrong**

The name, set, number, language, variant (for example holo or reverse), and condition suggested by CardFlow or by third-party tools may be incorrect. Printed language and promotional prints are easy to mix up.

**You must verify the card**

Before you buy, sell, trade, or inventory a card, you must check:

- Identity (name, set, card number)
- Variant and finish
- Language
- Condition
- Authenticity (counterfeits exist)

CardFlow does not authenticate cards and does not grade cards.

**Values are estimates, not appraisals or offers**

Any dollar figure, “market,” “comp,” or trend you see is an **estimate** based on third-party or incomplete data. It is:

- Not an appraisal
- Not an offer to buy or sell
- Not a guarantee of what you will pay or receive
- Not investment, tax, or legal advice

Markets move. Fees, shipping, taxes, and condition change the real result. Data may be stale, matched to the wrong variant, or missing.

**Max Buy is your number, and it can be wrong**

Max Buy (or any similar limit) is calculated from **your** settings (fees, target margin, and the estimate). If your settings or the estimate are wrong, Max Buy is wrong. It is not a recommendation that a purchase at that price is safe or profitable.

**No profit guarantee**

CardFlow does not promise that you will make money, avoid losses, win auctions, or sell inventory at any price.

**No automated bidding or purchasing**

CardFlow does not place bids, complete checkouts, or scrape live marketplace data for you. If you use Whatnot, eBay, or another site, you do that in their app or website, under their rules.

**Not affiliated**

CardFlow is an independent project. Unless a separate written agreement is shown to you, CardFlow is **not** affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or partnered with:

- Whatnot
- eBay
- The Pokémon Company or Nintendo
- CardSight
- TCGdex

Pokémon and related marks are the property of their owners. Catalog text and images from third parties may be unofficial and incomplete.

**Third-party services**

Recognition and some catalog or price data may come from CardSight, TCGdex, or others. Those services have their own terms. They may change, go down, or return errors. CardFlow is not responsible for their data.

# D14 premium (placeholder)

- **Prices (placeholder, no IAP yet):** $6.99/month or $59.99/year. Single constant: `PREMIUM_PLACEHOLDER_PRICING` in `packages/shared/src/premium.ts`.
- **Pre-grade estimates (Sean, Oct 18):** quick 2-photo estimates are free 10 a month, unlimited on premium. Full 14-photo grades: free 2 a month, premium 25 (docs/GRADE_FULL_MODE.md).
- **Reset date (Sean, Oct 18):** the 1st of each calendar month, 00:00 UTC, for everyone (the server has no user time zone; the app shows it as "Resets Nov 1"). Exception: an account with a billing date (`grade_accounts.billing_anchor_at`, set only once Apple billing exists) keeps resetting on that date so Premium matches Apple's billing period. `anchor_at` (account creation) no longer moves the reset. On deploy, usage counted under the old per-account period keys isn't carried over, so existing users get a fresh allowance once.
- **No rollover:** the monthly allowance (10 free; premium unlimited) resets on the reset date, and unused estimates don't roll over. Constant: `estimatesRollOver: false`.
- **Bought credits never expire (Sean, Oct 16, 2026; Apple guideline 3.1.1).** Credits from a credit pack stay on the account until they're used. They're spent only after the monthly allowance is used up, and they're never reset, voided or replaced. A later purchase adds to them. `credits_period` now records only the period of the last purchase. Constant: `creditsExpire: false`.
- **Credit packs:** 10 credits for $2.99 and 25 for $5.99, Premium-only (`PREMIUM_PLACEHOLDER_PRICING.creditPacks`). They'll be built after the Apple developer account exists. Until then nothing is for sale (`POST /v1/grade/credits` is a local/dev stub only), and the app doesn't show packs. Pack-screen copy is ready in `CREDIT_PACK_COPY`. **Decided (Sean, Oct 17):** if Premium lapses, leftover bought credits stay usable. Buying packs needs active Premium (`POST /v1/grade/credits` answers 403 `premium_required`, feature `credit_packs`, to free accounts). Spending leftover credits doesn't need Premium: on Free they're spent after the 10 free estimates.
- **Estimate history:** free accounts see their **10 most recent** estimates per card. Older ones stay saved on the server ("N older estimates saved, not deleted. Upgrade to see them."). Premium sees the full history. Importing old on-phone history is premium-only (403 `premium_required`); on a free account the phone keeps its copy.
- **Batch pre-grade:** premium-only. The batch screen checks `POST /v1/grading/batch` (a premium check that runs nothing), and each estimate still spends quota.
- **App:** the quota caption ends with "Resets Nov 15", shown in the phone's local time zone, and the out-of-estimates hint and the Premium prompt use the exact Policy Reviewer copy (`CREDITS_NEVER_EXPIRE_COPY`): "Your monthly allowance resets on your reset date and unused monthly estimates don't roll over. Credits you buy never expire."
- **Existing accounts (migration 0016 + `backfillQuotaAnchors`, before Oct 18):** anchored to the billing date if one exists, else the account creation date (`user_accounts.created_at`), else the earliest known activity (scans, sessions, inventory, history), else the first month with usage. The usage they already have this calendar month carries into the in-progress period, and bought credits are kept, so the deploy resets nobody. After that they reset monthly on their own anchor date, just like new accounts.
- **History:** free accounts see their 10 most recent estimates per card, and older ones are locked, not deleted. Premium history is paginated (50 per page, `before=nextCursor`, max 100 per page) instead of being capped.
- **Rollout note:** usage rows are now keyed by period start date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) instead of `YYYY-MM`, so everyone gets a fresh allowance at deploy.

- **Livestream scanner (Sean, Oct 19):** free for 3 days from account creation, then a subscribe paywall (`LIVESTREAM_PAYWALL_COPY` / `LivestreamPaywall`). Purchase goes through `startPremiumPurchase()` behind `EXPO_PUBLIC_CARD_FLOW_IAP_ENABLED` (off by default). While off, the button only shows "Subscriptions are opening soon." When on, show Apple 3.1.2 disclosures (price, period, auto-renew, Restore Purchases, Terms, Privacy). **TODO:** connect StoreKit 2 or RevenueCat; no new native modules until a dev build. Unlock remains `grade_accounts.plan` / `CARD_FLOW_PREMIUM_USER_IDS`.

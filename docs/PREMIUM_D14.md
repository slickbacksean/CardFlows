# D14 premium (placeholder)

- **Prices (placeholder, no IAP yet):** $6.99/month or $59.99/year. Single constant: `PREMIUM_PLACEHOLDER_PRICING` in `packages/shared/src/premium.ts`.
- **Pre-grade estimates:** free 5 a month, premium 30.
- **Reset date:** each user's own date. That's the billing date once billing exists (`grade_accounts.billing_anchor_at`), otherwise the account creation date (`anchor_at`, set when the account is first seen). Days past the end of a short month clamp, so a 31st anchor resets on Feb 28/29. Not the calendar month.
- **No rollover:** the monthly allowance (5 free, 30 premium) resets on the reset date, and unused estimates don't roll over. Constant: `estimatesRollOver: false`.
- **Bought credits never expire (Sean, Oct 16, 2026; Apple guideline 3.1.1).** Credits from a credit pack stay on the account until they're used. They're spent only after the monthly allowance is used up, and they're never reset, voided or replaced. A later purchase adds to them. `credits_period` now records only the period of the last purchase. Constant: `creditsExpire: false`.
- **Credit packs:** 10 credits for $2.99 and 25 for $5.99, Premium-only (`PREMIUM_PLACEHOLDER_PRICING.creditPacks`). They'll be built after the Apple developer account exists. Until then nothing is for sale (`POST /v1/grade/credits` is a local/dev stub only), and the app doesn't show packs. Pack-screen copy is ready in `CREDIT_PACK_COPY`. **Open:** if Premium lapses, are leftover credits kept but locked, or still usable?
- **Estimate history:** free accounts see their **10 most recent** estimates per card. Older ones stay saved on the server ("N older estimates saved, not deleted. Upgrade to see them."). Premium sees the full history. Importing old on-phone history is premium-only (403 `premium_required`); on a free account the phone keeps its copy.
- **Batch pre-grade:** premium-only. The batch screen checks `POST /v1/grading/batch` (a premium check that runs nothing), and each estimate still spends quota.
- **App:** the quota caption ends with "Resets Nov 15", shown in the phone's local time zone, and the out-of-estimates hint says unused estimates don't roll over (it no longer says anything about credits).
- **Existing accounts (migration 0016 + `backfillQuotaAnchors`):** anchored to the billing date if one exists, else the account creation date (`user_accounts.created_at`), else the earliest known activity (scans, sessions, inventory, history), else the first month with usage. The usage they already have this calendar month carries into the in-progress period, and bought credits are kept, so the deploy resets nobody. After that they reset monthly on their own anchor date, just like new accounts.
- **History:** free accounts see their 10 most recent estimates per card, and older ones are locked, not deleted. Premium history is paginated (50 per page, `before=nextCursor`, max 100 per page) instead of being capped.
- **Rollout note:** usage rows are now keyed by period start date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) instead of `YYYY-MM`, so everyone gets a fresh allowance at deploy.
